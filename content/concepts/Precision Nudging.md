# Precision Nudging

## Core idea

[[Precision Nudging]] uses data, machine learning, or AI to select which nudge, timing, format, or target is most likely to produce a desired behavioral outcome for a particular person or situation. It is a form of personalized nudging.

The key question in precision nudging is: which intervention will best move this person toward this target behavior?

[[Mills, 2022|Personalized Nudging (Mills)]] helps clarify what "precision" might mean. Precision can apply to delivery, such as timing or nudge type, or to content, such as the target behavior or default selected for a person.

## Contrast with Behavioral Economics 2.0

[[Ludwig et al., 2025|Algorithms as a Vehicle to Reflective Equilibrium: Behavioral Economics 2.0]] explicitly distinguishes its project from precision nudging. Precision nudging asks which treatment best nudges someone in a given direction. [[Behavioral Economics 2.0]] asks what direction is right for this person under [[Reflective Equilibrium]].

That contrast is important for the course. Precision nudging expands delivery precision; Behavioral Economics 2.0 tries to expand welfare precision.

Mills's framework keeps this contrast tidy. Precision nudging can personalize delivery or content, but it does not by itself solve the reflective-equilibrium problem of whether the selected target is actually right for the user.

## Relationship to hypernudging

Precision nudging overlaps with [[Hypernudge]] because both involve data-driven personalization and adaptation. The emphasis differs. Hypernudging, in Yeung's sense, foregrounds regulation by design and the risk of opaque control. Precision nudging foregrounds improved targeting, timing, and effectiveness.
