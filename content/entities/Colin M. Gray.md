# Colin M. Gray

## Who or what it is

Colin M. Gray is an HCI and design-ethics scholar who enters the vault through two central dark-pattern sources: [[Gray et al., 2018|The Dark (Patterns) Side of UX Design]] and [[Gray et al., 2024|An Ontology of Dark Patterns Knowledge]].

## Why it matters here

Gray's work anchors the taxonomy side of the dark-pattern module. The 2018 paper frames dark patterns as the co-opting of human-centered design values, while the 2024 ontology harmonizes categories across academic and regulatory work.
