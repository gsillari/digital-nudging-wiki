# Arunesh Mathur

## Who or what it is

Arunesh Mathur is a computer scientist and privacy/HCI researcher whose work in this vault focuses on dark patterns, online manipulation, and the measurement of exploitative interface design.

## Why it matters here

Mathur's work supplies both the empirical and normative spine of the dark-pattern cluster. [[Mathur et al., 2019|Dark Patterns at Scale]] documents dark patterns across shopping websites, while [[Mathur et al., 2021|What Makes a Dark Pattern... Dark?]] asks how to connect design attributes to welfare, autonomy, collective harms, and regulatory objectives.

- Linked to [[Dark Patterns]], dark pattern taxonomy ([[Dark Patterns]]), dark pattern regulation ([[Dark Patterns]]), and adversarial choice architecture ([[Dark Patterns]])
- Bridges empirical web measurement with normative and legal evaluation
