# George Loewenstein

## Who or what it is

George Loewenstein is a behavioral economist at Carnegie Mellon University and an important figure in behavioral economics, behavioral public policy, disclosure research, intertemporal choice, and the limits of choice-preserving policy.

## Why it matters here

In [[Loewenstein and Chater, 2017|Putting Nudges in Perspective]], Loewenstein and [[Nick Chater]] argue that nudges should be placed inside a broader policy toolkit. Behavioral economics can justify and improve nudges, but it can also justify traditional tools such as taxes, regulation, mandates, and subsidies when those tools better address the policy problem.

In [[Loewenstein et al., 2014|Disclosure: Psychology Changes Everything]], Loewenstein and coauthors show why disclosure often fails unless it is designed around attention, comprehension, motivation, and usable comparison.
