# David Lehr

David Lehr is a coauthor, with [[Cary Coglianese]], of the vault's foundational administrative-law papers on algorithmic governance. In this course, his work enters through the legal compatibility of machine-learning decision tools and the distinction between fishbowl and reasoned transparency.
