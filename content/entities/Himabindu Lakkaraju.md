# Himabindu Lakkaraju

## Who or what it is

Himabindu Lakkaraju is a computer scientist and coauthor of [[Kleinberg et al., 2018|Human Decisions and Machine Predictions]].

## Why it matters here

Lakkaraju enters the vault through the efficiency branch on algorithmic decision support in bail, where the central issue is whether machine predictions can improve judicial decisions under selective labels and omitted payoffs.
