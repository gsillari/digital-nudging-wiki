# Nick Chater

## Who or what it is

Nick Chater is a behavioral scientist at Warwick Business School whose work is relevant here through behavioral public policy and the diagnosis of how behavioral insights should be used in policy design.

## Why it matters here

In [[Loewenstein and Chater, 2017|Putting Nudges in Perspective]], Chater and [[George Loewenstein]] argue that behavioral economics should not be reduced to nudging. Their framework helps the course separate behavioral diagnosis from behavioral intervention and keeps nudges connected to traditional policy tools.

## Key relationships
