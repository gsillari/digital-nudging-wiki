# Karen Yeung

## Who or what it is

Karen Yeung is a legal scholar whose work connects regulation, technology, design-based control, Big Data, and algorithmic governance. In this vault, she is the author of [[Yeung, 2017|Hypernudge: Big Data as a Mode of Regulation by Design]].

## Why it matters here

Yeung supplies the course's key bridge from classic nudging to digital nudging. Her concept of [[Hypernudge]] makes personalization, recursive feedback, continuous modification, and algorithmic opacity central to the analysis of digital choice architecture.
