# Digital Nudging Wiki

This is the working knowledge base for the course **Digital Nudging**.

The first foundational source ingested is [[Thaler and Sunstein, 2021|Nudge: The Final Edition]], with selective unit notes for the chapters most useful to the course's opening vocabulary. The first practitioner quick reference is [[EAST Framework]]. The first dedicated ethics framework is [[Sunstein, 2016|The Ethics of Influence: Government in the Age of Behavioral Science]], complemented by [[Hansen and Jespersen, 2013|Nudge and the Manipulation of Choice]] for nudge definition and manipulation typology, and now synthesized in [[Nudging and the Ethics of Nudging]]. [[Yeung, 2017|Hypernudge: Big Data as a Mode of Regulation by Design]] is the first cornerstone digital-nudging source on structural risks, [[Ludwig et al., 2025|Algorithms as a Vehicle to Reflective Equilibrium: Behavioral Economics 2.0]] is the first programmatic source on constructive algorithmic support, and [[Mills, 2022|Personalized Nudging (Mills)]] supplies the delivery-versus-target personalization distinction. The dark-pattern branch is anchored by [[Gray et al., 2018|The Dark (Patterns) Side of UX Design]], [[Mathur et al., 2019|Dark Patterns at Scale]], [[Narayanan et al., 2020|Dark Patterns: Past, Present, and Future]], [[Mathur et al., 2021|What Makes a Dark Pattern... Dark?]], [[Luguri and Strahilevitz, 2021|Shining a Light on Dark Patterns]], and [[Gray et al., 2024|An Ontology of Dark Patterns Knowledge]], with [[Dark Patterns as Adversarial Choice Architecture]] as the synthesis. The algorithmic fairness branch now begins with [[Angwin et al., 2016|Machine Bias]], [[Kleinberg et al., 2016|Inherent Trade-Offs in the Fair Determination of Risk Scores]], [[Hedden, 2021|On Statistical Criteria of Algorithmic Fairness]], [[Hellman, 2020|Measuring Algorithmic Fairness]], [[Binns, 2018|Fairness in Machine Learning: Lessons from Political Philosophy]], [[Corbett-Davies et al., 2017|Algorithmic Decision Making and the Cost of Fairness]], and [[Narayanan, 2026|What If Algorithmic Fairness Is a Category Error?]]. The efficiency branch is anchored by [[Kleinberg et al., 2018|Human Decisions and Machine Predictions]] and [[Ludwig and Mullainathan, 2021|Fragile Algorithms and Fallible Decision-Makers]], with [[Mullainathan, 2025|Economics in the Age of Algorithms]] as the summary frame. The algorithmic-governance cluster adds [[Coglianese and Lehr, 2017|Regulating by Robot]], [[Coglianese and Lehr, 2019|Transparency and Algorithmic Governance]], [[Coglianese, 2021|Administrative Law in the Automated State]], [[Coglianese and Crum, 2024|Taking Training Seriously]], [[Coglianese and Crum, 2025b|Regulating Multifunctionality]], and [[Coglianese and Crum, 2025a|Leashes, Not Guardrails]] as the administrative-law and management-based-regulation layer.

## Starting Structure

- [[Digital Nudging]]: course-level topic hub
- [[Thaler and Sunstein, 2021|Nudge: The Final Edition]]: foundational source note
- [[Sunstein, 2016|The Ethics of Influence: Government in the Age of Behavioral Science]]: ethics framework for nudging and choice architecture
- [[Hansen and Jespersen, 2013|Nudge and the Manipulation of Choice]]: typology for responsible nudging, transparency, and manipulation
- [[Nudging and the Ethics of Nudging]]: synthesis of the foundational nudging definition and ethics framework
- [[Yeung, 2017|Hypernudge: Big Data as a Mode of Regulation by Design]]: digital pivot into Big Data-driven choice architecture, personalization, continuous modification, recommender systems, privacy, and algorithmic accountability
- [[Ludwig et al., 2025|Algorithms as a Vehicle to Reflective Equilibrium: Behavioral Economics 2.0]]: programmatic pivot into algorithmic thought partners, reflective equilibrium, adaptive armor, precision nudging, and Behavioral Economics 2.0
- [[Mills, 2022|Personalized Nudging (Mills)]]: systematic source for delivery personalization versus content or target personalization
- [[Gray et al., 2018|The Dark (Patterns) Side of UX Design]]: early HCI taxonomy for exploitative UX design
- [[Mathur et al., 2019|Dark Patterns at Scale]]: empirical crawl of dark patterns on shopping websites
- [[Narayanan et al., 2020|Dark Patterns: Past, Present, and Future]]: genealogy of dark patterns from retail deception, nudging, and growth hacking
- [[Mathur et al., 2021|What Makes a Dark Pattern... Dark?]]: normative and measurement framework for dark-pattern harms
- [[Luguri and Strahilevitz, 2021|Shining a Light on Dark Patterns]]: experimental and legal evidence on dark-pattern efficacy
- [[Gray et al., 2024|An Ontology of Dark Patterns Knowledge]]: harmonized dark-pattern ontology
- [[Dark Patterns as Adversarial Choice Architecture]]: synthesis of dark patterns as extraction-oriented digital choice architecture
- [[Algorithmic Fairness]]: sub-hub for the COMPAS controversy, fairness metrics, impossibility results, bias, efficiency, and prediction
- [[Angwin et al., 2016|Machine Bias]]: ProPublica's COMPAS investigation and opening algorithmic fairness case
- [[Kleinberg et al., 2016|Inherent Trade-Offs in the Fair Determination of Risk Scores]]: formal impossibility result for calibration and error-balance fairness criteria
- [[Hedden, 2021|On Statistical Criteria of Algorithmic Fairness]]: defense of calibration and predictive parity as fundamental criteria for fair prediction
- [[Hellman, 2020|Measuring Algorithmic Fairness]]: legal argument for prioritizing pragmatic error burdens over purely epistemic predictive parity
- [[Binns, 2018|Fairness in Machine Learning: Lessons from Political Philosophy]]: normative bridge from technical fairness metrics to philosophical theories of discrimination, equality, and justice
- [[Corbett-Davies et al., 2017|Algorithmic Decision Making and the Cost of Fairness]]: policy-alignment source on fairness constraints, public goals, and decision costs
- [[Narayanan, 2026|What If Algorithmic Fairness Is a Category Error?]]: sociotechnical corrective to metric-centered fairness
- [[Kleinberg et al., 2018|Human Decisions and Machine Predictions]]: efficiency-frontier source for algorithmic bail decision support
- [[Ludwig and Mullainathan, 2021|Fragile Algorithms and Fallible Decision-Makers]]: implementation source for fragile algorithms, selective labels, omitted payoffs, and human overrides
- [[Mullainathan, 2025|Economics in the Age of Algorithms]]: overview source for prediction policy problems and algorithms as empirical, intervention, and theory tools
- [[Ludwig et al., 2024|The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Algorithms]]: effectiveness source for algorithmic policy interventions, MVPF, ranking gains, and scale
- [[Predictive Algorithms, Bias, and Fairness]]: synthesis of the prediction, bias, fairness, efficiency, and sociotechnical-system threads
- [[Coglianese and Lehr, 2017|Regulating by Robot]]: legal-compatibility source for machine-learning adjudication and rulemaking
- [[Coglianese and Lehr, 2019|Transparency and Algorithmic Governance]]: source for fishbowl transparency, reasoned transparency, and algorithmic reason-giving
- [[Coglianese, 2021|Administrative Law in the Automated State]]: automated-state source on expertise, accountability, and empathy
- [[Coglianese and Crum, 2024|Taking Training Seriously]]: source on human-guided training and management-based AI regulation
- [[Coglianese and Crum, 2025b|Regulating Multifunctionality]]: source on foundation-model heterogeneity and multifunctionality
