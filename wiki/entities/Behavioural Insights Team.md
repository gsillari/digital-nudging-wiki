# Behavioural Insights Team

## Who or what it is

The [[Behavioural Insights Team]] is an applied behavioral science organization associated with the development and diffusion of behavioral insights in policy and practice. In the EAST handbook, BIT presents itself as the organization that originally launched the [[EAST Framework]] in 2014 and revised it in 2024.

## Why it matters here

BIT is important for the Digital Nudging course because it represents the practitioner side of behavioral public policy: diagnosing behavioral barriers, designing interventions, testing them, and scaling what works. Its EAST framework gives the vault a compact design checklist for practical behavioral interventions.

## Key relationships

- Publisher of [[Behavioural Insights Team, 2024|EAST: Four Simple Ways to Apply Behavioural Insights]]
- Developer of the [[EAST Framework]]
- Connected to [[Nudge]], [[Choice Architecture]], [[Behavioral Economics]], and evaluation-oriented behavioral public policy
