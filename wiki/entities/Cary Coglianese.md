# Cary Coglianese

Cary Coglianese is a legal scholar of regulation, administrative law, and algorithmic governance. In this vault, he anchors the administrative-law and AI-governance cluster: legal compatibility of algorithmic administration, transparency and reason-giving, the automated state, and management-based regulation for heterogeneous AI.
