# Brian Hedden

## Who or what it is

Brian Hedden is a philosopher and author of [[Hedden, 2021|On Statistical Criteria of Algorithmic Fairness]].

## Why it matters here

Hedden provides the course's first explicit philosophical defense of calibration or predictive parity as a fundamental fairness condition for predictive algorithms.
