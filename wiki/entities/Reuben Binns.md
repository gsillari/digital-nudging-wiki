# Reuben Binns

## Who or what it is

Reuben Binns is a researcher in algorithmic fairness and author of [[Binns, 2018|Fairness in Machine Learning: Lessons from Political Philosophy]].

## Why it matters here

Binns provides the course's anchor for treating algorithmic fairness as a normative and political-philosophical problem rather than merely a statistical optimization problem.
