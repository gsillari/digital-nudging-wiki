# Arvind Narayanan

## Who or what it is

Arvind Narayanan is a computer scientist and scholar of algorithmic accountability, privacy, machine learning ethics, and dark patterns. In this vault, he enters through [[Narayanan, 2026|What If Algorithmic Fairness Is a Category Error?]], [[Mathur et al., 2019|Dark Patterns at Scale]], and [[Narayanan et al., 2020|Dark Patterns: Past, Present, and Future]].

## Why it matters here

Narayanan supplies the strongest current corrective to metric-centered algorithmic fairness in the course sequence. His contribution is to reframe fairness as a property of sociotechnical decision systems and algorithmic bureaucracies rather than of predictive models alone.

In the dark-pattern module, Narayanan's coauthored work helps frame dark patterns as a convergence of behavioral nudging, retail deception, growth hacking, and A/B-tested extraction.
