# Jamie Luguri

## Who or what it is

Jamie Luguri is a legal scholar who enters the vault through [[Luguri and Strahilevitz, 2021|Shining a Light on Dark Patterns]].

## Why it matters here

Luguri's coauthored work provides experimental evidence that dark patterns can materially shift consumer behavior and a legal argument for treating many dark patterns as unfair or deceptive practices.

## Key relationships

- Linked to [[Dark Patterns]], [[Dark Pattern Regulation]], and [[Adversarial Choice Architecture]]
- Important for the empirical claim that mild dark patterns can be effective without triggering backlash
