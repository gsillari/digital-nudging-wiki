# Richard H. Thaler

## Who or what it is

Richard H. Thaler is a behavioral economist and coauthor, with [[Cass R. Sunstein]], of [[Thaler and Sunstein, 2021|Nudge: The Final Edition]]. In the book's front matter, he is described as the 2017 Nobel Memorial Prize recipient for contributions to behavioral economics and as a University of Chicago Booth School of Business professor.

## Why it matters here

Thaler supplies much of the behavioral economics lineage behind [[Nudge]], including work on mental accounting, self-control, status quo bias, and practical choice architecture. His role matters for Digital Nudging because the course inherits a design-oriented version of behavioral economics rather than a purely descriptive one.
